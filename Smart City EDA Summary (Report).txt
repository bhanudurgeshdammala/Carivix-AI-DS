EDA Summary (Report)
Dataset Structure
Total observations: 5,000
Total features: 14
The dataset includes 4 categorical variables, 4 integer variables, and 6 floating-point variables.
Each record represents a traffic observation at a specific time and geographic location, including traffic flow, environmental conditions, and operational metrics.
Feature Analysis
Traffic-related variables include vehicle count, traffic speed, road occupancy, traffic light state, and traffic condition.
Environmental variables include weather condition, emission levels, and energy consumption.
Operational metrics include accident reports, ride-sharing demand, and parking availability.
Timestamp data enables temporal analysis of hourly and daily traffic patterns.
Distribution Analysis
The dataset contains no missing values, ensuring complete records for all observations.
Vehicle count and road occupancy exhibit moderate variability, reflecting fluctuations in traffic density.
Traffic speed generally decreases as vehicle count and road occupancy increase, indicating congestion effects.
Emission levels and energy consumption vary across observations and are positively associated with traffic volume.
Correlation analysis highlights meaningful relationships among vehicle count, traffic speed, occupancy, emissions, and fuel consumption, making the dataset suitable for traffic forecasting, congestion analysis, and intelligent transportation system applications.
