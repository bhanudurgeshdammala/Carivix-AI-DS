EDA Summary (Report)
Dataset Structure
Total observations: 740
Total features: 30
The dataset contains 2 categorical variables (state and district names) and 28 numerical variables representing employment, labour budget, wages, expenditure, and work progress under the MGNREGA scheme.
Each row corresponds to a district, providing district-level statistics.
Feature Analysis
The dataset includes employment-related metrics such as job cards issued, active workers, households worked, and individuals employed.
Social inclusion indicators include SC workers, ST workers, and differently abled persons worked.
Financial variables capture approved labour budgets, wages paid, total expenditure, and administrative expenditure.
Infrastructure and project-related features include ongoing works, completed works, and total works taken up.
Distribution Analysis
Most numerical variables exhibit right-skewed distributions, with a few districts reporting substantially higher employment and expenditure than others.
Wage rates are relatively concentrated compared to expenditure-related variables.
Boxplots reveal outliers in expenditure and workforce metrics, reflecting variations in district size and program implementation.
Correlation analysis indicates strong positive relationships between employment measures, expenditure, and work execution, suggesting that districts with larger workforces generally report higher spending and greater numbers of completed projects.
The dataset contains no missing values, making it suitable for further statistical analysis and predictive modeling without requiring data imputation.
